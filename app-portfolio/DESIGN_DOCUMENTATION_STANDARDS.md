# Design Documentation Standards

Project documentation shall state its purpose, scope, audience, and evidence. Distinguish observed behavior, assumptions, proposed decisions, and unresolved questions.

Use stable requirement identifiers and trace requirements to design, implementation, and verification evidence. Describe component boundaries, interfaces, data models, quality attributes, rationale, and tradeoffs. Use diagrams where they clarify the system.

Keep source attribution intact. Preserve references and licensing obligations for borrowed material. Format deliverables for their intended audience and apply the agreed citation standard where required.

Review documents for factual accuracy, complete sentences, reproducible commands, and consistency with the current product baseline. Do not present plans or prototypes as shipped capabilities.
